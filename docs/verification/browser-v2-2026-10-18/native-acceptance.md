- Settings: traverse the original categories, search, Command+F and numbered
  navigation at narrow and wide window sizes. Confirm full category labels and

  focused editor, transient menu, dirty draft and a held key.

  sample practices, mark known, undo and snooze. Repeat with learning disabled.
  Verify no unsolicited tip appears without a complete safety snapshot.

  Toggle either opt-in off while the panel is open and confirm revocation.

## Geometry, sessions and input

  canonical five-pane arrangement, swap two panes, apply, resize, maximize,
  restore and Undo/Redo. Cancel each preview and drag, including IME Escape,

- Repeat with a real two-party audio/video call, screen share and an edited
  document. Run the reviewed 30-minute / 100-action continuity matrix. Verify
  no hidden capture is evicted, navigated, replaced or paused by the browser.
- Save a layout, change it, and inspect every previous/proposed Update field.
  Concurrent saved revisions must invalidate a pending review. Cold restore

  joining or media permission request. Verify lazy bounded startup separately.

- Trigger repeated page-fade attempts and redirects. Confirm the per-tab URL
